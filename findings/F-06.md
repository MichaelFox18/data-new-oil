# F-06  The exhaust premium — platforms earn $7–56/yr per user from data worth pennies

CLAIM (one sentence, specific, falsifiable):
  The big ad platforms earn roughly $7 to $56 in revenue per active user per year
  (Meta ~$56 per daily user; Reddit ~$18; Snap ~$13; Pinterest ~$7 per monthly user),
  while a person's raw data sells on the broker market for a fraction of a dollar — an
  "exhaust premium" of roughly two to three orders of magnitude. (Google earned $402.8B
  with no per-user metric disclosed.)
