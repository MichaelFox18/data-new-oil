# F-09  Hubbert nailed the 1970 oil peak — then fracking broke his forecast 12x over

CLAIM (one sentence, specific, falsifiable):
  M. King Hubbert's 1956 forecast correctly predicted the 1970 peak of US crude
  production (actual conventional peak 1970 at 3.52 Gb/yr, matching his 200-Gb curve), and
  output declined roughly as he projected to a 2008 trough (1.83 Gb/yr) — but then fracking
  reversed it: 2025 US production hit an all-time record 4.96 Gb/yr, about 12x what
  Hubbert's curve forecast for that year. The scarcity prophet was right for ~40 years,
  until a new extraction technology made a fool of the forecast.

TYPE:        MEASURED (actual production, EIA) + MODELED (Hubbert-curve reconstruction) +
             REPORTED (Hubbert's 1956 parameters).
NUMBERS:     Actual US crude (EIA): 1970 conventional peak 3.52 Gb/yr; 2008 trough 1.83
             Gb/yr; 2025 all-time record 4.96 Gb/yr. Hubbert 200-Gb curve at 2025 = 0.41
             Gb/yr -> actual is ~12x his forecast. Hubbert params (REPORTED): ultimate
             150 Gb (peak ~1965) and 200 Gb (peak ~1970), lower-48.
SOURCES:     SRC-009 EIA US field production of crude oil (Tier A, MEASURED actual);
             SRC-010 Hubbert 1956 parameters (Tier B REPORTED; primary = his API paper).
METHOD:      src/acquire/eia.py (EIA API, key redacted) + src/analyze/hubbert.py — actual
             in Gb/yr; Hubbert curve reconstructed as logistic-derivative
             P(t)=Pmax*sech^2((t-tm)/tau), tau=Q/(2*Pmax). results/hubbert.json.
             Figure: src/viz/hubbert.py -> figures/hubbert.png.
COVERAGE:    US total crude 1859-2025 (plotted 1900+); Hubbert curve 1900-2025.
CONFIDENCE:  High for actual (EIA). The reconstructed Hubbert 200-Gb curve tracks actual
             closely from 1900 to ~1970, which both validates the reconstruction and shows
             how good his call was — then the shale divergence is unambiguous in the data.
NOVELTY:     Supporting / Chapter-6 spine, not a novel discovery: "Hubbert vs actual" is
             widely shown. The value is a clean recompute from EIA + a faithful curve, and
             the sharp "12x his forecast / record in 2025" framing.

             - TOTAL US (EIA NUS) vs Hubbert's LOWER-48: Alaska (Prudhoe Bay, 1977+) adds a

             - Curve reconstruction is approximate: peak rates ~2.5/3.0 Gb/yr are his
               curve's stated peaks; the sech^2 form is the standard Hubbert logistic.
             - "Crude oil" definition (field production) vs crude+condensate/NGLs.
