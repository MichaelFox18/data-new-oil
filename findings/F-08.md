# F-08  The deferral overlay — synthetic data buys only ~3 years per 10x

CLAIM (one sentence, specific, falsifiable):
  Modeling synthetic data as multiplying the effective training-data pool, the data wall
  slides right only ~3.1 years for every 10x of data added — because frontier training
  grows ~2.2x/year — so 10x synthetic pushes the wall to ~2032, 100x to ~2035, and even
  1000x the entire human-text corpus only reaches ~2038: synthetic data defers the wall
  but cannot abolish it without exponential supply (and model collapse threatens its
  quality).

TYPE:        MODELED. Inputs: frontier trend MEASURED (SRC-001), human-text stock REPORTED
             (SRC-008). The synthetic multiplier M is an explicit ASSUMPTION (a sweep), not
             a prediction or a measurement.
NUMBERS:     Deferral leverage = 3.14 years per 10x of effective data (= 1 / frontier
             OM-per-year). MODELED crossover by synthetic multiplier M:
             - M=1 (human only) -> ~2029.1
             - M=3   -> ~2030.6
             - M=10  -> ~2032.2
             - M=30  -> ~2033.7
             - M=100 -> ~2035.4
             - M=1000 -> ~2038.5
SOURCES:     SRC-001 Epoch frontier trend (Tier A, MEASURED); SRC-008 Epoch stock (Tier A,
             REPORTED). M has NO source — it is swept, not estimated. The pitch's "Gartner:
             synthetic overtakes real data by 2030" is REPORTED context to attribute, not
             used as an input here.
METHOD:      src/analyze/deferral.py — effective stock = human stock (~4e14, growing
             0-10%/yr) x M; solve the frontier trend == effective stock for each M.
             results/deferral.json. Figure: src/viz/deferral.py -> figures/deferral.png.
COVERAGE:    A what-if sweep over M in {1,3,10,30,100,1000}; not a forecast of real
             synthetic-data volume.
CONFIDENCE:  The LEVERAGE (~3 yr per 10x) is robust — it follows directly from the measured
             frontier slope and is insensitive to the exact stock level. The ACTUAL
             deferral is unknowable: it depends on real synthetic volume and on how fungible
             synthetic data is with human data.
NOVELTY:     "Synthetic data defers the wall" is a common claim (Gartner et al.). The
             original cut is quantifying the LEVERAGE from first-party data: reframing
             "synthetic saves us" into "synthetic buys ~3 years per 10x, and you need
             exponentially more of it" — which sets up the climax tension directly.
ALTERNATIVES / confounders:
             - MODEL COLLAPSE (REPORTED, research literature): training on a model's own
               output degrades quality, capping the effective multiplier M. This is the
               counter-force and Chapter 7's open question — NOT quantified here.
             - Fungibility: synthetic data is unlikely to be 1:1 as useful as human data, so
               these crossovers are UPPER bounds on the deferral a given M buys.
             - A steeper/shallower frontier slope scales the leverage proportionally.
             - Multimodal/non-text data is a separate lever, not modeled here.
ROBUSTNESS:  Leverage = 1 / (frontier OM-per-year) — a direct consequence of the MEASURED
             slope; scenarios are deterministic. Sensitivity: halving/doubling the frontier
             growth rate halves/doubles the years-per-10x.

             effective-stock lines and their crossover years marching right; leverage
             annotated. Fed by results/crossover.json + results/deferral.json.
