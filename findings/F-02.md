# F-02  The four-state broker census — 803 unique, 166 register everywhere

CLAIM (one sentence, specific, falsifiable):
  Merging and name-deduplicating the four public state data-broker registries
  (California, Vermont, Oregon, Texas) yields 803 unique data brokers; 166 of them
  (20.7%) are registered in all four states and 437 (54%) in two or more — a core of
  national "professional" brokers that the state-by-state lists obscure.

TYPE:        MEASURED
NUMBERS:     Per-state unique brokers (after exact-dup drop + name normalization):
             - CA 581 (raw 581) | VT 438 (raw 714, -26 exact-dup rows) |
               OR 309 (raw 352) | TX 386 (raw 397)
             Naive sum of per-state uniques ... 1,714
             NATIONAL UNIQUE brokers (dedup) .... 803
             Removed by cross-state dedup ....... 911
             Overlap (registered in N states): 1->366, 2->129, 3->142, 4->166
             In all four states: 166 (e.g. Acxiom, Claritas, Catalina Marketing,
             Babel Street, Blackbaud, 6sense, AnalyticsIQ, Pipl, Civis Analytics).
SOURCES:     SRC-002 (CA), SRC-003 (VT), SRC-004 (OR), SRC-005 (TX) — all Tier A.
             Triangulated across four independent government registries (Y).
METHOD:      src/analyze/broker_census.py — per-state name column extracted (CA "Data
             broker name:", VT "Name", OR "full_name", TX "Full Legal Name" via dynamic
             header detection past the export's preamble + blank line); exact-duplicate
             rows dropped; names normalized (casefold, strip punctuation + conservative
             legal-form suffixes inc/llc/corp/ltd/lp/co/...); cross-state set union.
             Outputs: results/broker_census.json, data/interim/brokers_normalized.csv.
COVERAGE:    Each state's current public registrant list (CA 2026 cycle; TX export dated
             2026-06-04; OR current; VT bulk export may include multiple registration
             years). Self-registration regimes -> a coverage floor, not all brokers.
CONFIDENCE:  Medium-High. The computation is clean and deterministic; the main
             uncertainty is name matching.
NOVELTY:     CORRECTED by prior-art check (2026-06-04). A cross-state merge is NOT new:
             Privacy Rights Clearinghouse + EFF (reported by CyberScoop, Apr 2025) merged
             ~5 state registries into ~750 unique data-broker GROUPS, focused on
             non-registration gaps. So "first national count" would be insight theater.
             Defensible contribution is narrower: (1) a June-2026 refresh on current
             snapshots that TRIANGULATES with their ~750 (our 803 name-level entities is
             consistent — they group by corporate parent, which lowers the count);
             (2) the OVERLAP cut — 166 brokers registered in all four states, a national
             "core", vs their gaps angle; (3) the bridge to CA's GenAI-sharing flag (F-01).
             Headline = the all-four core + the 2026 triangulation, NOT a "first".
ALTERNATIVES / confounders:
             - Name-spelling variance cuts both ways: under-merging across states makes
               803 a near-CEILING on the unique count and 166-in-all-4 a FLOOR on overlap
               (fuzzy matching would only raise overlap). Over-merging risk checked: the
               shortest in-all-4 names (ixi, bdex, pipl, azira) are real broker brands.
             - States define "data broker" and set thresholds differently, so per-state
               counts are not perfectly comparable; the union and overlap are the robust
               figures, not state-vs-state size.
             - VT export likely carries multi-year/inactive registrations; name-dedup
               collapses repeats but the 438 may mix active + historical (DataBrokerStatus
               to be used for an active-only cut in a follow-up).
             - Subsidiaries vs. parents counted as distinct entities.
ROBUSTNESS:  In-all-4 names spot-checked = real national brokers (not collisions);
             deterministic re-run from raw; planned sensitivity: fuzzy/threshold matching
             to bound how much 803/166 move. EXTERNAL TRIANGULATION: PRC/EFF's ~750
             corporate "groups" (Apr 2025, ~5 registries) corroborates our 803 name-level
             count given their parent-level grouping — independent method and date.
