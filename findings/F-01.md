# F-01  California brokers' self-reported collection & GenAI-sharing

CLAIM (one sentence, specific, falsifiable):
  Of 581 data brokers registered with California (CPPA) in the 2026 cycle, 31 (5.3%)
  self-report having sold or shared consumers' data with a developer of a GenAI system
  or model in the past year; 110 (18.9%) report collecting precise geolocation and 18
  (3.1%) report collecting minors' personal information.

TYPE:        MEASURED
NUMBERS:     n = 581 registered brokers. Share self-reporting "Yes":
             - precise geolocation ........ 110 (18.9%)
             - gender identity ............  68 (11.7%)
             - sold to federal government .  52 ( 9.0%)
             - sold to other state govts ..  50 ( 8.6%)
             - sold to a GenAI developer ..  31 ( 5.3%)   <- headline
             - sold to law enforcement ....  27 ( 4.6%)
             - sold to a foreign actor ....  26 ( 4.5%)
             - minors' personal info ......  18 ( 3.1%)
             - sexual orientation .........  18 ( 3.1%)
             - biometric ..................  11 ( 1.9%)
             - citizenship/immigration ....  11 ( 1.9%)
             - reproductive health ........   7 ( 1.2%)
             - union membership ...........   6 ( 1.0%)
SOURCES:     SRC-002 CPPA Data Broker Registry (Tier A). Triangulated? N — single
             registry, but it is the brokers' own primary self-report to the regulator,
             not an aggregator. TX (SRC-005) has a comparable minors flag for a future
             cross-check (see F-02 line of work).
METHOD:      src/analyze/ca_categories.py — loads data/raw/cppa/registry.csv (utf-8),
             matches each category column by case-insensitive substring (robust to the
             curly apostrophes in official headers), counts "Yes", share = Yes / 581.
             Output: results/ca_categories.json.
COVERAGE:    All 581 brokers in the 2026 CA registration cycle. These flags are 0% blank
             (profiled), so no imputation. EXCLUDES brokers that did not register
             (self-registration regime = a coverage floor, not all brokers).
CONFIDENCE:  High for the computation (clean Yes/No, 0% blank, deterministic).
             Medium for interpretation — see ALTERNATIVES (self-report).
NOVELTY:     Preliminary. The GenAI-sharing share is from a column that appears new to the
             2026 form and is, to my knowledge, not yet published — likely genuinely
             novel. The geolocation/minors shares may have prior-year analogs from privacy
             groups. PRIOR-ART CHECK PENDING before GATE 6 (§11): confirm whether anyone
             has published the 2026 shares, especially the GenAI cut.
ALTERNATIVES / confounders:
             - Self-report bias: brokers likely UNDER-report sensitive collection, so
               these are lower bounds, not upper. State as "self-report at least X%".
             - "Sold to a GenAI developer" is the broker's own characterization of a
               counterparty; definition may vary across filers.
             - Registration-cycle timing: 2026 cycle reports on 2024 activity for some
               metrics; confirm the reference period for the sharing questions.
             - Coverage floor: non-registrants and brokers below thresholds are absent.
ROBUSTNESS:  0% blank on all reported columns; script re-runs deterministically from raw;
             spot-check a handful of rows by hand against the raw CSV before VERIFIED.
